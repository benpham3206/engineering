# measurement

Knowing how well you know something. Every number needs a unit and an uncertainty.

**Prerequisites:** high-school algebra, or [math](../math/README.md) for the algebra and basic calculus used in uncertainty and fitting.

## Topics

- [Units and dimensional analysis](01-units-and-dimensional-analysis.md)
- [Precision, accuracy, and uncertainty](02-precision-accuracy-and-uncertainty.md), then [propagating uncertainty and significant figures](03-propagating-uncertainty-and-significant-figures.md)
- [Metrology tools: calipers, micrometers, gauges](04-reading-calipers-and-micrometers.md)
- [Sensors and transducers](05-sensors-and-transducers.md), then [strain gauges and the Wheatstone bridge](06-strain-gauges-and-wheatstone-bridge.md) and [thermocouples and accelerometers](07-thermocouples-and-accelerometers.md)
- [Data acquisition and signal conditioning](08-data-acquisition-and-sampling.md)
- [Experiment design](09-experiment-design.md)
- [Data analysis and reporting](10-data-analysis-and-reporting.md)

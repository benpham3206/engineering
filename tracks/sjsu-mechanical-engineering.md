# SJSU mechanical engineering track

**Type:** track

This maps one real program's course sequence, San Jose State University mechanical engineering, onto the shared lessons in this repo. The general track, with no school attached, is [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

Each course lists the lessons and reference sheets that cover it, in the order to study them. "Not covered yet" lines mark parts of a course the repo has no lesson for.

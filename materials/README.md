# materials

What things are made of, why they behave the way they do, and how to choose the right one.

## Topics

- Atomic structure and bonding
- Crystal structures and defects
- Mechanical properties: strength, stiffness, hardness, toughness, ductility
- Phase diagrams and heat treatment
- Metals, polymers, ceramics, composites
- Failure: fracture, fatigue, creep, corrosion
- Material selection

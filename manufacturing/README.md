# manufacturing

How parts actually get made, and what that means for how they should be designed.

## Topics

- Shop safety
- Machining: milling, turning, drilling, CNC
- Additive manufacturing
- Casting, forming, and molding
- Joining: welding, fastening, adhesives
- Sheet metal
- Tolerances, fits, and surface finish
- Automation and robotics

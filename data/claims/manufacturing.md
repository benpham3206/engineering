# Manufacturing claims

**Type:** data

Facts about manufacturing practice that are not derived from physics: process capability ranges, typical tolerances and finishes by process, design rules from public guides, and shop practice. Capability ranges move as machines improve, so most claims here use layer `dated` with a `Review by` date about two years ahead; long-settled methods use `durable`. Field rules are in [VERIFICATION.md](../../VERIFICATION.md#claims).

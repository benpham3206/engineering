# SJSU claims

**Type:** data

Facts about San Jose State University's shops and makerspaces, used only by the [SJSU track](../../tracks/sjsu-mechanical-engineering.md). Equipment, hours, and access rules change from term to term, so every claim is dated with a short review period. Field rules are in [VERIFICATION.md](../../VERIFICATION.md#claims).

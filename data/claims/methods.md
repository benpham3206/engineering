# Method claims

**Type:** data

Durable engineering methods that are not derived from physics alone: empirical correlations, design curves, rules of thumb, and attributed methods that have held for decades. Each heading is a claim key with layer `durable`; a reference entry whose basis is empirical cites its claim in the `Source and basis:` line. Timeless results derived from physics or mathematics are not registered here. Field rules are in [VERIFICATION.md](../../VERIFICATION.md#claims).

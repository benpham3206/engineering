# Roadmap

This is one path through the repo for someone learning from scratch. It takes four years and follows the order of a mechanical engineering degree. Working engineers can skip it and use the reference sheets in each folder.

Each semester is about 15 weeks at 15-20 hours a week. Go faster or slower as you need. The order matters more than the pace.

Every semester has three parts:

- Study the lessons from the folders listed.
- Build at least one project from [applications](applications/README.md).
- Write a short report on what you built, using [communication](communication/README.md).

## Before you start

You need high-school algebra and trigonometry. If those are rusty, start with the first lessons in [math](math/README.md) and come back.

Also read these once at the start, and again every year:

- [philosophy](philosophy/README.md): what engineering is and the responsibility that comes with it
- [problem-solving](problem-solving/README.md): the method you will use on every problem in this repo

## Year 1: foundations

**Semester 1**

- [math](math/README.md): algebra and trig review, single-variable calculus
- [physics](physics/README.md): statics
- [tools](tools/README.md): set up CAD and a programming environment, using the [free software guide](tools/09-free-software.md)
- [design](design/README.md): sketching and engineering graphics

**Semester 2**

- [math](math/README.md): multivariable calculus, intro to linear algebra
- [physics](physics/README.md): dynamics
- [design](design/README.md): CAD parts, assemblies, and drawings
- [manufacturing](manufacturing/README.md): shop safety, hand tools, 3D printing
- [measurement](measurement/README.md): units, significant figures, calipers and micrometers

## Year 2: the core sciences

**Semester 3**

- [math](math/README.md): differential equations, linear algebra
- [physics](physics/README.md): mechanics of materials
- [materials](materials/README.md): structure, properties, metals and polymers
- [physics](physics/README.md): electricity and magnetism

**Semester 4**

- [physics](physics/README.md): waves, light, heat
- [materials](materials/README.md): phase diagrams, heat treatment, failure
- [manufacturing](manufacturing/README.md): machining, joining, sheet metal
- [applications](applications/README.md): circuits

## Year 3: thermal-fluid sciences and design

**Semester 5**

- [physics](physics/README.md): thermodynamics, fluid mechanics
- [math](math/README.md): numerical methods and software solvers
- [measurement](measurement/README.md): uncertainty, sensors, data acquisition
- [communication](communication/README.md): technical reports

**Semester 6**

- [physics](physics/README.md): heat transfer
- [design](design/README.md): tolerancing, GD&T, machine elements, factor of safety
- [materials](materials/README.md): material selection
- [applications](applications/README.md): mechatronics
- [reverse-engineering](reverse-engineering/README.md): teardowns and recreating parts in CAD

## Year 4: systems and capstone

**Semester 7**

- [physics](physics/README.md): vibrations and controls
- [math](math/README.md): probability and statistics
- [applications](applications/README.md): robotics, thermal and fluid systems
- [manufacturing](manufacturing/README.md): tolerances, fits, automation
- [design](design/README.md): design for manufacturing and assembly

**Semester 8**

- Capstone: design, build, test, and document one real project end to end. Use [design](design/README.md) for the process and [applications](applications/README.md) for project ideas.
- [philosophy](philosophy/README.md): ethics, safety, and global impact, revisited with four years of experience
- [communication](communication/README.md): final report and presentation

## Checking yourself

You are ready to move on from a semester when you can:

- Solve the practice problems in each lesson without looking at the worked example
- Explain each core idea out loud to someone who has not studied it
- Point to something you built that uses it

If you want an outside benchmark, the NCEES FE Mechanical exam covers about the same material as Years 1-3.
